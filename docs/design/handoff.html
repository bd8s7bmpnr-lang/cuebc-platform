Design decisions

  • Annual reuse: conference library, create/copy, archive and year-scoped settings. Copying excludes attendees, payments, private meeting URLs and consent history.
  • Registration: information → ticket → review → payment. Workshop selection is conditional: open choice, personal schedule, or capacity-backed reservation.
  • Payments: draft, processing, declined and paid are distinct. Only verified payment confirms a paid ticket. Retries must be idempotent. Receipts have view, download and resend paths.
  • Waitlists: conference and workshop queues are separate. An offer holds availability temporarily; an offer is not a paid registration. Expired offers release capacity.
  • Program: content publication is separate from timetable publication. Room, presenter and capacity conflicts block schedule publication.
  • Privacy: dietary/accessibility fields have restricted access and are excluded from default exports. Every privileged action is audited.
  • Conference day: fast lookup, duplicate detection, walk-in registration, verified payment and manual arrival status. QR passes/scanning and presenter self-service are later-phase features.

Shared visual specification

See the visual system screen and shared stylesheet. Navy #102F46; blue #08769E; orange #BB4B18; surface #F4F7F9. System sans-serif, 16px body, 1.55 line height, 8px spacing rhythm, 12px cards and visible keyboard focus. Primary controls are at least 44px high; compact table actions must retain adequate target spacing.

Desktop artboards are 1440px wide. Mobile artboards are 390px wide. Public screens stack into a single column; organizer navigation collapses into a menu. Wide tables and timetable boards scroll within their containers.
